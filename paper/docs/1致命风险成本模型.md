# 论文1：UAV path optimization with an integrated cost assessment model considering third-party risks in metropolitan environments

## 3.1. Fatality risk cost model（致命风险成本模型）

### 概述

Fatal risk cost is defined as the cost that UAV crashes to pedestrians and persons in ground vehicles, and the unit is the number of fatalities per flight hour. The fatality risk cost is denoted as $c_{rf}$, and it consists of the fatality risk cost to pedestrians $c_{rp}$ and to the person in the vehicle $c_{rv}$.

$$c_{rf} = c_{rp} + c_{rv} \quad (1)$$

---

## 3.1.1. Fatality risk to pedestrians（行人致命风险）

UAVs may lose control and crash to pedestrians on the ground. To evaluate the fatality risk cost to pedestrians, there are three processes need to be considered:
1. System failure of UAV
2. UAV crashes to pedestrians
3. Fatal damages caused to the pedestrians

That falling UAV hits people on the ground causing fatalities is a sequential action, which associates with the three processes mentioned above. The risk cost of UAV crashes to pedestrians $c_{rp}$ is defined as the number of fatalities per flight hour, denoted as:

$$c_{rp} = P_{crash} N_{hit}^p R_f^p \quad (2)$$

where:
- $c_{rp}$ is the risk cost associated with the fatality of pedestrians
- $P_{crash}$ is the probability of UAV system failure
- $N_{hit}^p$ is the number of pedestrians hit by crashed UAV (correlated with population density)
- $R_f^p$ is the fatality rate that people killed in UAV accidents

The $P_{crash}$ is primarily determined by the reliability of the UAV system itself, including hardware and software reliabilities. The $R_f^p$ is correlated with the weight and falling height of the UAV. The uncertain variable in Eq. (2) is $N_{hit}^p$, which is associated with the population density, defined as:

$$N_{hit}^p = S_{hit} \sigma_p \quad (3)$$

where:
- $S_{hit}$ is the size of UAV crash impact area
- $\sigma_p$ is the population density in the administrative unit

### Fatality rate（致命率）

The fatality rate $R_f^p$ associated with two main factors: impact kinetic energy and sheltering effects. The kinetic energy $E_{imp}$ of falling UAV primarily determines the severity of impact, while the sheltering coefficient $S_c$ affects the degree of impact on the people and vehicle, as the buffering effects of buildings and trees will soften the impact. 

The sheltering coefficient $S_c$ is introduced as the absolute real number $S_c \in (0, 1]$, and the fatality rate is presented as:

$$R_f^p = \frac{1}{1 + \sqrt{\frac{\alpha}{\beta}} \left(\frac{\beta E_{imp}}{}\right)^{\frac{1}{4}} S_c} \quad (4)$$

where:
- $\alpha$ is the impact energy that might cause 50% fatality with $S_c = 0.5$
- $\beta$ is the impact energy threshold required to cause fatality as $S_c$ approaching zero

Based on that, we take $\alpha = 10^6$ J and $\beta = 100$ J.

### Impact kinetic energy（冲击动能）

The impact kinetic energy $E_{imp}$ of the falling UAV is known as:

$$E_{imp} = \frac{1}{2} m v^2 \quad (5)$$

where:
- $m$ (kg) is the mass of the falling UAV
- $v$ is the velocity when the UAV hits the ground

### Vertical drag force（垂直阻力）

The vertical drag force $F_d$ of falling UAV is related to its size and materials, as well as the density of air, etc., denoted by:

$$F_d = \frac{1}{2} R_I S_{hit} \rho_A v_{TAS}^2 \quad (6)$$

where:
- $R_I$ is the drag coefficient that is related to the UAV type
- $\rho_A$ is the density of air (1.225 kg/m³ at sea level)
- $v_{TAS}$ is the true airspeed of the falling UAV

### Acceleration（加速度）

The acceleration of UAV can be then presented as:

$$a = \frac{F_g - F_d}{m} = g - \frac{R_I S_{hit} \rho_A v_{TAS}^2}{2m} \quad (7)$$

where $F_g$ is the gravitational force, $F_g = mg$ ($g = 9.8$ m/s²).

### Ground impact velocity（落地速度）

Thus, the velocity $v$ of UAV hits the ground at moment $t$ can be obtained as:

$$v = \int_0^t \left(g - \frac{R_I S_{hit} \rho_A v_{TAS}^2}{2m}\right) dt = \sqrt{\frac{2mg}{R_I S_{hit} \rho_A} \left(1 - e^{-\frac{h R_I S_{hit} \rho_A}{m}}\right)} \quad (8)$$

where $h$ is the falling height of the UAV above ground level.

## 3.1.2. Fatality risk to persons in the vehicle（车内人员致命风险）

Fatality risk cost to persons in the vehicle is modelled and there are also three components of a crash UAV accident on vehicles:
1. UAV system failure
2. Falling UAV hits a vehicle
3. The crash accident causes a direct fatality to people or causes a traffic accident which subsequently kills persons in the vehicle

Fatality risk cost that UAV crashes to persons in the vehicles is defined as the number of fatalities per flight hour, denoted as:

$$c_{rv} = P_{crash} N_{hit}^v R_f^v \quad (9)$$

where:
- $N_{hit}^v$ is the number of vehicles hit by the crashed UAV
- $R_f^v$ is the average fatality rate that persons in vehicles are killed in accidents, which can be obtained by: (annual total fatalities caused by car accidents) / (annual total number of car accidents)

The average number of vehicles that might be hit by crashed UAV can be defined as the ratio of the total projected area of all vehicles and the total road area, denoted as:

$$N_{hit}^v = S_{hit} \sigma_v \quad (10)$$

in which:
- $S_{hit}$ is the size of UAV crash impact area
- $\sigma_v$ is the vehicle density in a road network
